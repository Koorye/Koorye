**Embodied Intelligence Researcher** at **Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence (BAAI)**. I received my M.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from the **University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)**.

My research focuses on large-scale manipulation data and embodied foundation models: intent-aware egocentric and bimanual datasets, vision-language-action adaptation, and vision-language model transfer. I have authored **7 papers**, including **CVPR**, **ICLR**, **ICRA**, and **IJCV**, with **300+ citations**; my released datasets are used by **360+ institutions** and have surpassed **100,000,000 cumulative downloads**.

## Research Profile

- **Large-scale manipulation data** — first-person and bimanual datasets, intent-aware acquisition, hierarchical annotation.
- **Embodied foundation models** — vision-language-action adaptation, test-time inference, and spatial reasoning.
- **Vision-language model transfer** — prompt tuning, parameter-efficient learning, and contrastive objectives.

Current work centers on **HumanCOIN** (12,500 hours of egocentric manipulation data, 38 scene classes, 30 institutions, with gaze/sEMG/EEG intent tiers) and **RoboCOIN** (20 institutions, 15 embodiments, 180K+ demonstrations).
